District 1

Home to the Marikina Sports Center and Clock Tower, serving as the city's bustling civic and commercial hub.
Sto. Niño
A historic riverside barangay known for its strong community spirit and vibrant local festivals.
Malanday
A major gateway to the city featuring a dynamic mix of commercial spots and residential subdivisions.
Barangka
A lively community near the Riverbanks Center, offering a convenient mix of schools, businesses, and leisure spots.
San Roque
The site of the first Catholic mass in Marikina, known for its deep historical roots and old-town charm.
Jesus Dela Peña
A tight-knit community near the city center, connecting the riverbanks to major thoroughfares.
Tañong
A peaceful riverside community known for the San Antonio de Padua Church and accessible transport routes.
Kalumpang
A planned community with organized streets and open spaces, offering a quiet suburban feel near the river.
Industrial Valley Complex
The heart of Marikina's heritage, housing the OLA Parish and the Kapitan Moy ancestral house.

District 2

A major hub featuring the Concepcion Public Market and a vibrant mix of commerce and residences.
Concepcion Uno
A vibrant residential community along the river, known for its resilience and local urban farming.
Tumana
A quiet residential area known for upscale subdivisions and proximity to the Lilac St. food district.
Concepcion Dos
An elevated, suburban barangay filled with parks, schools, and spacious subdivisions.
Marikina Heights
The northernmost barangay featuring a mix of industry and residences bordering San Mateo.
Nangka
A sprawling area hosting key city institutions and a mix of traditional and modern neighborhoods.
Parang
An economic driver for the city, balancing major industrial plants with quiet residential enclaves.
